---
name: Help Desk scope
description: Why operational help is deliberately separate from paid SBU expert chat.
---

Keep the floating Help Desk focused on operating the application using curated
instructions. Substantive certification questions belong in the source-backed
SBU assistant, with its own consent, quota and verification limits.

**Why:** The user requested a guide to the application, features and usage.
Sending those support questions into certification consultation would mix
product instructions with legal inference, incur avoidable AI cost and invite
unnecessary private-document input.

**How to apply:** Do not silently replace the local support guide with a paid
model or grant access to private files. A future AI upgrade needs explicit
scope, consent and budget design. Support chat must not claim to inspect an
account, fix permissions, verify a certificate or contact a human operator.
