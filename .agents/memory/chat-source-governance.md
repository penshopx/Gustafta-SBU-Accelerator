---
name: Chat source governance boundaries
description: Why chat retrieval approval stays independent from transition verification.
---

Treat approval to retrieve a curated preparation summary as a separate human
decision from legal validity or verification of regulatory transition triggers.
Replacement content requires code review as well as a runtime source decision;
reviewer authority alone must not introduce arbitrary regulatory content.

**Why:** The chatbot corpus deliberately includes bounded, unverified preparation
summaries. Equating retrieval approval with legal verification would silently
raise the strength of those claims or enable the withheld BG001 draft rules.

**How to apply:** Preserve this separation when extending source management.
Do not connect withdrawal/replacement decisions to transition dates, rule
activation, or publisher authentication without an explicit new requirement.
