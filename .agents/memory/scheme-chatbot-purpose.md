The user wants chatbots for each construction classification and sub-classification,
guided by the supplied scheme documents and KBLI reference. They should help
admin BUJK, verifikator/peninjau, asesor, and pelaksana LSBU understand the schemes.

**Why:** The user stated this product goal explicitly.

**How to apply:** Keep scheme and sub-classification context explicit and support
the different audiences. This goal is not permission to grant private case access
or make official certification decisions.

Do not assume the uploaded KBLI book can be redistributed in full commercially.
Use bounded paraphrased explanations and document/page references pending a
rights review, rather than publishing the complete book.

**Why:** The supplied BPS publication includes a written-permission restriction
for commercial reproduction. Uploading a reference does not settle reuse rights.

**How to apply:** Recheck the actual license/permission before adding a public
download or full-book reader; do not conflate this with bans on explaining codes.
