The user explicitly endorsed withholding SBU transition dates until a written, verifiable publisher source is available, and not substituting a socialization invitation for the operative letter. When no eligible information requester exists, document the attempted public-source verification and a ready-to-submit request, but leave the publication status unknown rather than asserting non-issuance. The user also confirmed that negative gating is only the defensive half of the requirement: it must not be described as a completed positive flow for displaying dates after official verification.

**Why:** The user confirmed this conservative compliance decision was appropriate even though it leaves official certainty unresolved, and explicitly accepted the distinction between withholding an unverified date and enabling a verified one.

**How to apply:** For future changes that could expose transition dates, require publisher evidence first and preserve the distinction between "not publicly located" and "not issued." Do not treat appreciation or provisional closure as proof that the missing official verification happened. Report defensive tests separately from positive verified-source behavior.
