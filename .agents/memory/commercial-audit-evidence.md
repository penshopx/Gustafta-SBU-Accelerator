---
name: Commercial audit evidence
description: Status definitions and limits for SBU commercial-readiness assessments.
---

Use **Terbukti**, **Belum Terverifikasi**, and **Belum Tersedia** in commercial-readiness reports. Every proven claim must identify its evidence boundary: code implementation, automated test, live Preview, production, or real-user validation. Missing evidence is not proof that a capability is absent.

**Why:** Earlier audit drafts conflated an implemented export with an untested browser flow, an unexamined recovery procedure with unavailable backups, and incomplete live isolation testing with a demonstrated leak. These distinctions change commercial decisions.

**How to apply:** Split feature availability from verification where necessary. Evaluate regulatory completeness against the scope actually offered to buyers, not every possible subclassification. Describe preparation-time benefits separately from official SBU issuance timelines. Give pilot, limited paid launch, and general-sale recommendations separately; do not invent completion dates.
