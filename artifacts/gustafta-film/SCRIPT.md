# Gustafta — Satu sumber, akses terarah

## Treatment
30.00 seconds, Indonesian, **16:9**. Audience: construction businesses preparing
new or renewal SBU applications. Promise: organize preparation around one company
data source, with explicit authorization boundaries.

Identity: existing SBU project logo (public/logo.svg), unchanged; Manrope display,
DM Sans supporting face, Space Mono labels. Project navy #101f32, bright blue
#82c8ff, cobalt #1769d1, white #f4f9ff; these are project-derived, not an assertion
about a separate upstream brand guide. Hero: company profile sheet / folder.
Motion: sheet assembly, scale match cuts, traced authorization paths, folder
boundary reveal. Arc: fast SBU hook, source consolidation, measured permission
explanation, exclusion demonstration, preparation recap, light-field ending.
Payoff: original logo on a light field, rather than another dark feature frame.
No VO/SFX requested. One restrained electronic instrumental, 108 BPM, no vocals;
beat synchronization is not claimed.

## Review
All 30 seconds covered. Six compositions differ in framing and dominant
action, not just their headlines. Sheet/folder/path carriers connect cuts.
Supporting type ≥3vmin; primary headlines ≥7vmin; 6% safe inset. No empty
incoming frame; subject stays visible during tails. Final brand hold is a
deliberate exception to the one-beat-per-second floor. No live connection,
active folder feature, government affiliation, official authenticity, PDF
verification, upload-in-SBU, approval guarantee, or automatic certification
claims. Every illustration is noninteractive; controls are iframe-only.
